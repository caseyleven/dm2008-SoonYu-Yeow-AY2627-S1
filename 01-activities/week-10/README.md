In the sketch Kapi provided, you could change the colour, shape, and size of a shape. I wanted more ways to modify the shape, so I added specific dials like redness, greenness, and blueness to adjust the 'r', 'g', and 'b' of the shape. Of course, the slider values were from 0-255. Also, I made the control panel look a bit more presentable.
